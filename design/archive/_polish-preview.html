Option A

Unify focus treatment

Two focus idioms today — a leftover outline on links/buttons and the signature box-shadow ring on inputs. After unifies everything to the milled-bezel ring so focus reads as one control everywhere.

Option B

Refine the status rail

Option C

Dark-mode contrast to AA

In dark mode, --ink-3 on --sheet is ~3.9:1 — just under WCAG AA for the small mono labels that use it. After nudges --ink-3 and --rule-2 up a few percent. Switch to dark mode to see the right column brighten to AA.

Option D

Selection + scrollbar polish
