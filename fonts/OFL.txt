Fonts bundled in this directory
==============================

yazeed.blog self-hosts its three typefaces instead of loading them from
Google Fonts, so the page makes no third-party request. Every file here was
downloaded from Google Fonts' own CDN (fonts.gstatic.com) and is shipped
unmodified. All three families are licensed under the SIL Open Font License,
Version 1.1, the full text of which follows this header.

  unmodified; any modified version must be renamed without using "Plex".

All six files are the "latin" subset Google serves, carrying the unicode-range
declared in index.html's @font-face block. The Big Shoulders and IBM Plex Mono
files are static instances at the weight in their filename; libre-franklin-var
is the variable font (wght 100-900), pinned per @font-face declaration.
